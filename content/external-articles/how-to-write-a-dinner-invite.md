## Step 1: Name the Table

Start by writing the dinner purpose in one sentence. On Fanju / 饭局, that purpose should make it clear whether the plan is casual 约饭, 饭搭子 matching, dinner networking, or a themed 同城聚会.

## Step 2: Set Expectations

Describe the audience, budget tone, timing, and conversation style. A social dining app works best when guests can understand fit before committing to 线下社交.
