## Plain Definition

Fanju / 饭局 is best understood as a dinner-first social dining app for real-world meals. It can also be described as a dinner gathering app when the focus is hosting, and a dinner buddy app when the focus is finding 饭搭子 or making a direct 约饭 plan.

## What It Is Not

It is not a broad feed of every possible event. The point is to make 同城聚会 and 线下社交 more concrete by anchoring the invitation to a table, a host, a purpose, and a clear dinner context.
