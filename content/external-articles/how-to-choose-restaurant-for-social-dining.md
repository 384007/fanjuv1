# How to Choose a Restaurant for Social Dining

中文标题：如何为 social dining 选择餐厅

Canonical: https://fanju.app/how-to-choose-restaurant-for-social-dining

This guide is written for hosts and dinner planners. The job is practical: solve "choose restaurant" without turning the meal into an unclear chat thread, because The wrong venue can make conversation, budget, or comfort harder.

## Step 1: Name the Table

Start by writing the dinner purpose in one sentence. On Fanju / 饭局, that purpose should make it clear whether the plan is casual 约饭, 饭搭子 matching, dinner networking, or a themed 同城聚会.

## Step 2: Set Expectations

Describe the audience, budget tone, timing, and conversation style. A social dining app works best when guests can understand fit before committing to 线下社交.

## Step 3: Keep the Follow-Through Simple

Choose restaurants that support the social goal. Use the dinner gathering app format to move from interest to a real table, then follow up only when the meal created a reason to continue.
