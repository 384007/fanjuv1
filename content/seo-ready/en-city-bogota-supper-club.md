## FAQ

What kind of food can I expect at a Bogota supper club?
Expect a wide variety, reflecting Bogota’s rich culinary landscape. Hosts often specialize in traditional Colombian dishes, regional specialties, or international cuisines they are passionate about. Some supper clubs might focus on vegetarian or vegan options, while others might explore fusion concepts or specific cooking techniques. The Fanju app listings will detail the planned menu, giving you a clear idea of what to anticipate.

What is the typical cost of attending a supper club?
The cost varies depending on the host, the complexity of the menu, and the ingredients used. Prices generally reflect the value of the meal and the host’s time and effort. You can find the exact price per person clearly stated in the event listing on the Fanju app before you decide to book.

What should I wear to a supper club?
Dress codes can vary, but most supper clubs lean towards smart casual. It’s always best to check the event description for any specific recommendations from the host. Generally, aim for an outfit that is comfortable yet presentable, allowing you to relax and enjoy the evening without feeling out of place. Think of it as dressing for a nice dinner with friends.

Can I bring a guest to a supper club?
Supper clubs are typically designed for a specific number of guests to maintain an intimate atmosphere. If you wish to bring someone, you usually need to book spots for both of you when you register. Some hosts might have specific policies regarding plus-ones, so it’s advisable to check the event details or contact the host through the Fanju app if you have any questions about bringing a guest.

What if I have dietary restrictions or allergies?
Many hosts are accommodating, but it’s crucial to communicate any dietary restrictions or allergies well in advance. You can usually find information on whether a host can cater to specific needs in the event description. If not, it’s best to message the host directly through the Fanju app before booking to ensure they can safely accommodate you. Transparency is key for a positive experience for everyone.
