# Vienna Table Tennis Dinner Guide

A Vienna table tennis dinner is for people who enjoy ping pong, light sports, and casual conversation after a game or around a shared interest. Fanju app keeps the plan dinner-first: topic, public venue, group size, cost, host note, and expectations are visible before joining.

## Who this dinner is for

It suits casual players, students, newcomers, and people who want a low-pressure way to meet local friends. The goal is not a tournament, but a clearer first table.

## What to check before joining

Check whether there is a playing session, where the group meets, whether beginners are welcome, and how dinner cost is shared.

## Practical boundaries

Respect different skill levels, do not turn the table into a competition unless stated, and keep the first meetup public.

## FAQ

### Do I need to be good at table tennis?

No, if the host welcomes casual players.

### Is this mainly dinner or sport?

It is dinner-first unless the listing clearly says there is a game session.
