# Zurich Productivity Dinner Guide

A Zurich productivity dinner is a small-table Fanju dinner for people who want focused conversation around work rhythm, habits, and practical routines.

## Who this is for

It suits professionals, students, founders, remote workers, and people who prefer a focused dinner table over a broad group chat.

## What to check

Check the public venue, table size, cost sharing, and whether the host explains the productivity topic clearly.

## FAQ

### Is this a workshop?

No. It is dinner-first social dining.

### Can I join alone?

Yes, when the table plan and public venue are clear.
