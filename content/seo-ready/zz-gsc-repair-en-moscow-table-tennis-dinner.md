# Moscow Table Tennis Dinner Guide

A Moscow table tennis dinner is a small-table Fanju dinner for people who want light sports conversation and a clear offline table.

## Who this is for

It suits casual players, newcomers, students, and people who prefer a focused table over a broad group chat.

## What to check

Check whether there is a play session, whether beginners are welcome, where the public venue is, how many people join, and how cost is shared.

## FAQ

### Do I need to be a strong player?

No, if the host welcomes casual players.

### Is this mainly dinner or sport?

It is dinner-first unless the host states otherwise.
