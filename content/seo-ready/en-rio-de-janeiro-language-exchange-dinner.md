Guests should also verify the exit plan: does the venue close at 23:00, or will the group need to find a later‑opening spot?  Knowing the exit timing helps participants coordinate safe transportation, especially when crossing from Lapa to the waterfront where late‑night traffic can be unpredictable.  If the venue details are vague, the dinner may be not for everyone who values clarity.
