# Guangzhou Curated Dinner Guide

A Guangzhou curated dinner is a small-table Fanju dinner with a clearer topic, host note, venue, group size, cost, and timing before joining.

## Who this is for

It suits people who want a more focused dinner than a broad group chat or casual meetup.

## What to check

Check the public venue, guest mix, cost sharing, table size, and whether the host explains the dinner theme clearly.

## FAQ

### Is this a dating event?

No. It is dinner-first social dining.

### Can I join alone?

Yes, when the table plan is clear and the venue is public.
