# Seoul Pottery Dinner Guide

A Seoul pottery dinner is for people who enjoy ceramics, craft, design, and calm cultural conversation. Fanju app keeps the plan dinner-first: topic, venue, group size, cost, host note, and expectations are visible before joining.

## Who this dinner is for

It suits pottery learners, designers, museum visitors, makers, students, and people who want a quieter table than a broad social meetup.

## What to check before joining

Check whether the dinner includes a studio visit, a show-and-tell, or only conversation. Confirm venue, timing, group size, and cost before joining.

## Practical boundaries

Respect different skill levels, do not pressure others to share personal work, and keep the first table public and clear.

## FAQ

### Do I need pottery experience?

No, if the host welcomes beginners.

### Is this a workshop?

Not necessarily. It is a dinner-first social table unless the host states a workshop format.
