### What should I check before joining my first table?

Look beyond the menu. Read the host’s description carefully—do they mention their cooking experience, their reasons for hosting, or how they manage dietary needs? Check recent guest notes for mentions of pacing, comfort, or conversation flow. Make sure the location fits your mobility and comfort level, especially in older neighbourhoods with narrow stairs or limited lighting. Confirm the end time, as Madrid nights run long, and know whether drinks are included. Most importantly, ask yourself if the table’s tone matches your mood—if it feels like a stretch, it’s okay to wait for one that doesn’t.
