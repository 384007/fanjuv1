# Casablanca Language Exchange Dinner Guide

A Casablanca language exchange dinner is for people who want language practice in a relaxed offline setting. Fanju app keeps the plan dinner-first: topic, public venue, group size, cost, host note, and expectations are visible before joining.

## Who this dinner is for

It suits language learners, students, remote workers, and people who enjoy cultural exchange. A small table is usually easier for real conversation than a large group chat.

## What to check before joining

Check which languages are expected, whether beginners are welcome, how the table will rotate conversation, and whether the venue is public and convenient.

## Practical boundaries

Respect accents and learning pace. Do not pressure anyone to share private details or join follow-up plans.

## FAQ

### Do I need to be fluent?

No, if the listing welcomes your level. Ask the host when unclear.

### Is this only for language students?

No. Anyone who fits the table theme can join.
