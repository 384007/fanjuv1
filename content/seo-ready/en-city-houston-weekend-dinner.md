In Houston, where neighborhoods can feel isolated despite proximity, the app begins the work of connection long before the meal, by clarifying intent: this is a space to listen, to share, to be present. That clarity is what allows the experience to feel different from a social media meetup or a crowded bar. The app’s design reflects this. They tell potential guests what kind of space they’re entering, and whether they’ll fit.
