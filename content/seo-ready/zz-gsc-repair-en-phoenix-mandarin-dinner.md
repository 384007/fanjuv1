# Phoenix Mandarin Dinner Guide

A Phoenix Mandarin dinner is for people who want to practice Mandarin, meet Chinese-speaking locals, or share Chinese culture through a small dinner table. Fanju app keeps the plan clear before joining: topic, venue, group size, cost, host note, and expectations.

## Who this dinner is for

It suits Mandarin learners, bilingual families, students, professionals, and people who want a calmer alternative to a large language group.

## What to check before joining

Check whether beginners are welcome, which language level is expected, whether the venue is public, and how the cost is shared.

## Practical boundaries

Respect different language levels and do not turn the dinner into a test. Keep the first table public and simple.

## FAQ

### Do I need fluent Mandarin?

No, if the host welcomes your level.

### Is this a class?

No. It is a dinner-first social table, not a formal lesson.
